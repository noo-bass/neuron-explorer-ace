Six songs, each planned once, then rendered again with one push at a time. Neuron 5455 (highlighted) sits beside another neuron and four random directions of exactly the same size, so you can hear whether 5455 does something the others don't.

What to listen for

  • Songs with piano (lo-fi, jazz trio, ballad, solo piano): does the piano drop out with 5455, and not with the random rows?
  • Songs without piano (rock band, ambient synth): does 5455 remove some other instrument, or nothing in particular?
  • Push −0.3 pushes the opposite way. If 5455 is a piano switch, this might make the piano louder or add one.
  • Push 0.15 is half the size of the push you heard before, to see where the effect starts.
  • "moved" is how different the clip is from the untouched one, as a fraction of its volume. It measures size, not what changed.
