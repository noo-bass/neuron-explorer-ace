What one push does to a song sheet

The planner is the part of ACE-Step that reads your request and writes a song sheet: tempo, key, beats per bar, length and a written description. Here we nudged one of its neurons, or a random direction of the same size, and let it write the sheet again. Nothing was turned into audio yet.

Push sizeMeasured as a share of the planner's usual internal signal at this layer. Minus pushes the other way. 0 is the untouched sheet.
Neuron vs randomRandom pushes are the yardstick. A neuron only counts as meaningful if it changes things in a more specific way than they do.
Highlighted wordsWords in a pushed description that are not in the untouched one, so you can see what moved.

The short version

"Broken" means the description stopped being normal English prose: other alphabets mixed in, or fewer than 15 words. Every one of the 128 pushed sheets changed in some way, even at the smallest size.
